# "Only manage balances in local currency" (SKB1-XSALH)

**Level:** 301 · for FI consultants configuring G/L accounts with open item management

**One line:** One checkbox on the G/L master decides whether an open item posted in USD is remembered as USD or only as its yen equivalent — and therefore whether clearing it later at a different rate produces an exchange rate difference or nothing at all.

## What the indicator does

*Only balances in local currency* is a field on the company code segment of a G/L account (FS00, *Control data* tab; SKB1-XSALH). When it is set, every posting to the account updates the transaction figures **in the local currency only**: the line's update currency (BSEG-PSWSL) is the company code currency and its update amount (BSEG-PSWBT) is the local-currency amount, whatever the document currency was. The balance of the account in GLT0, FAGLFLEXT or ACDOCA-TSL therefore has exactly one currency bucket. See [Update currency](../update_currency_pswsl/README.md) for the mechanism.

Without the indicator, the transaction figures are kept per document currency, and the account's balance display shows a bucket per currency it was ever posted in.

## Where it is set, where it must be, where it cannot

SAP's own guidance, from the field help and the support-content page on the indicator:

| Account | Indicator |
|---|---|
| Cash discount clearing accounts | **must** be set |
| GR/IR clearing accounts | **must** be set |
| Customer and vendor reconciliation accounts | **cannot** be set (open items have to be tracked in their own currency; KBA 3270987) |
| Balance sheet accounts not managed on an open item basis and not kept in foreign currency | usually set |
| Open-item accounts that receive the same kind of item in different currencies, where clearing should succeed whenever the *local* amounts match | usually set |
| Everything else | optional |

The GR/IR account is the clearest case. A purchase order in USD is received (credit GR/IR at the PO rate) and invoiced (debit GR/IR at the invoice rate); the two are cleared by F.13 against each other. Nobody wants a USD balance on GR/IR, nobody wants an exchange rate difference from the automatic clearing, and the indicator is what prevents both. Foreign currency valuation of GR/IR is its own subject (support-content page *Foreign Currency Valuation for WRX GRIR Clearing Account*).

## The example: one item, two clearings

The source document for this chapter walks through a JPY company code (Z013) with account 414100C managed on an open item basis, and a 100 USD item posted when 1 USD = 100 JPY. Two versions of the item exist, one on an account with the indicator and one without; both have DMBTR = 10,000 JPY. Then each is processed in F-03 when the rate is 1 USD = 110 JPY.

**With the indicator (document 1800000200):** F-03 shows the item as 10,000 JPY. The local amount is not recalculated; the account only ever knew a JPY amount, and 10,000 JPY is what it is worth. The item clears against 10,000 JPY of anything with no difference.

**Without the indicator (document 1800000196):** F-03 shows the item as 11,000 JPY. The account knows a USD amount, and to clear it in JPY today the system translates 100 USD at today's rate. Cleared against a second item of 10,000 JPY (document 1800000201, posted in JPY), the two do not balance: there is 1,000 JPY left over, the clearing screen opens on the *Residual items* tab with `1.000-` beside the USD item, and the choices are *Charge off diff.*, a residual item, or a partial payment. Whichever is chosen, the system posts the 1,000 JPY automatically as an exchange rate difference to the account configured in OBA1 for transaction key KDF. See [Exchange rate differences on clearing](../exchange_rate_differences_on_clearing/README.md).

The raw table displays in the source show `DMBTR 100,00` for both items, because JPY has no decimals and the field is stored shifted; see [Currency keys](../currency_keys_and_decimals/README.md). The program below reproduces every number and shows the SE16 view beside the real one.

## T001-XSLTA: the company-code-level switch

There is a second indicator, on the company code rather than the account: *No forex rate diff. when clearing in LC* (OBY6, T001-XSLTA). When it is set, a clearing that is *entered in local currency* does not recalculate the local amounts of the foreign-currency items being cleared; the items are taken at their DMBTR and no exchange rate difference is posted. It is the answer to "we clear USD items in JPY all day and do not want a difference each time" without giving up the per-currency balance on the account. KBA 2249505 defines its scope (it applies only to clearings in local currency, and only to the first local currency; a clearing entered in USD still revalues), and the support-content page on T001-XSLTA is the reference. The two indicators combine as follows:

| SKB1-XSALH | T001-XSLTA | Clearing entered in JPY, USD item at a new rate |
|---|---|---|
| set | any | item taken at DMBTR; no difference |
| not set | not set | item revalued at the clearing rate; difference posted |
| not set | set | item taken at DMBTR; no difference; USD bucket remains on the account |

Message F5263 (*Difference too large for clearing*) and its KBAs (2220851 has the flowchart) are where the interaction usually surfaces.

## Changing the indicator on a live account

The indicator changes the meaning of every past line's PSWSL, so the system refuses to change it while the account has a balance (message FH085, KBA 3423090) and the classic procedure is: bring the balance to zero in every currency, change the flag, post the balance back. For P&L accounts the flag has a further effect on the balance carried forward per currency (KBAs 2393152 and 3057269). In migrations to the new G/L, an account whose flag was changed mid-life shows up as an inconsistency in FAGL_ACTIVATE_OP (KBA 1858966).

Foreign currency valuation also reads the flag: an open-item account with *only balances in local currency* has no foreign-currency open items to valuate, and FAGL_FC_VAL silently does nothing for it (KBA 3138607).

## How to verify this in your own system

| Claim | Where to prove it |
| :-- | :-- |
| The indicator | `FS03`, *Control data*; `SKB1-XSALH` |
| Its effect on the update currency | `SE16N` on `BSEG` for a foreign-currency line of the account: `PSWSL` equals the local currency |
| It cannot be set on a reconciliation account | `FS00` on a reconciliation account, set it, read the message (KBA 3270987) |
| The recalculated amount at clearing | `F-03` in local currency on a USD item after the rate moved: the local-currency gross column, with and without the indicator |
| The company code switch | `OBY6`, *No forex rate diff. when clearing in LC*; `T001-XSLTA` |
| Changing it on an account with a balance | `FS02`, change the indicator, read the message (KBA 3423090); `FAGLB03` for the balance per currency first |
